# Micromentor – Product Assumptions

## 1. What product are you planning to build?

Micromentor is a website where students who don't understand something in class can find resource people and book a short 30-minute meeting with them to get their doubt solved. It is similar to Upwork and Fiverr, but instead of freelancers, it has resource people you can learn from.

## 2. What problem does it solve?

Students often can't clear their doubts with teachers or friends, because they may not have expertise in that particular field. Resource people who work in that field have deep knowledge of the topic. So Micromentor helps students who are working on a topic and want deeper, expert-level understanding of it.

## 3. Who are the target users?

The main users are college and engineering students who are working on a project or topic and want advice from resource people who are experts in that field. The other users are the resource people themselves, who offer short sessions to share their knowledge.

## 4. What is the main use case?

The main use case is helping students get their project checked or get an expert's opinion from resource people on Micromentor. For example, a student stuck on their DSA project opens Micromentor, filters mentors by the subject "DSA", opens a mentor's profile, picks a free 30-minute slot, confirms the booking, and then sees the session in "My Bookings".

## 5. What are the core features?

- Mentor profile cards showing name, field, workplace, subjects, rating and price
- Search and filter mentors by field, college subject and price
- Booking slots in a grid, like a cinema ticket booking app
- A confirmation popup before the booking is made
- "My Bookings" page to see and cancel booked sessions
- Login and register for students

## 6. What assumptions are you making about the users?

- Students working on a project often get stuck or miss something important, and need someone experienced to point it out.
- Students prefer talking to a real expert over asking AI, because a person can understand their exact problem and give trusted advice.
- Students learn better from a real person than a bot, because the mentor can see their reactions and notice when they're still confused.
- Students like connecting with professionals in their field, which also helps them build useful contacts.

## 7. What will be included in your MVP?

- A list of resource people (mentors) shown as profile cards, like freelancers on Upwork and Fiverr
- A filter to search mentors by the subject the student wants to learn, and by price
- A slot booking page where students pick a time slot, like choosing seats in a cinema booking app
- A confirmation popup and a "My Bookings" page to see or cancel sessions
- Simple login and register pages
- Dummy data for mentors instead of a real database

## 8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?

- Real online payment (the app only shows the price and total)
- Video call, phone call or chat with the mentor
- Mentors signing up and adding their own sessions (mentors come from dummy data)
- Real login and saving data (any email works, and bookings are lost on refresh because there is no backend)
- Ratings and reviews from students after a session
